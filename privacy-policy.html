Line War Kingdoms - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Line War Kingdoms (the "game") uses, why, and who it is shared with. The game is made by an independent developer in Norway ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact us at linewarkingdoms@gmail.com.

We do not ask for your real name, email address, location or contacts, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored only on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, unlocked races and abilities, whether you bought REMOVE ADS), your settings (including the player name you type in), campaign saves and the last address you joined in direct online play. These files stay on your device and are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

2. Online play (Epic Online Services)

Online matches, matchmaking and the ranking list use Epic Online Services, run by Epic Games. When you play online, the game logs in with an anonymous ID created for your device (no Epic account needed) and sends:

  • the player name you chose, shown to other players and on the ranking list;
  • your rating, used for matchmaking and the ranking list;
  • game moves during a match, sent to the other player;
  • technical data needed to connect, such as your IP address.

Epic processes this data under its own privacy policy: epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy. Please do not use your real name or anything offensive as your player name.

In direct play (joining by code or address on a home network or VPN), your device connects straight to the other player's device, which can see your IP address.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you buy REMOVE ADS, the game shows a banner in the menus and lets you watch optional video ads for rewards. The ads come from Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and (if you allow it) personalise them.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads load, and you choose whether ads may be personalised. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads). How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Purchases (Google Play)

REMOVE ADS is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation that the purchase was made, so the game can remove ads and restore the purchase on a new device.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

We do not keep a database of players ourselves. Your player name and rating on the ranking list are stored by Epic Online Services. To have them removed, email us at linewarkingdoms@gmail.com and we will delete them within 30 days. Data held by Google for ads and purchases is managed through your Google account.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and Norway), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Norway: Datatilsynet). Contact us to use these rights.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
